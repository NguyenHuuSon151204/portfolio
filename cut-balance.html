Cut Balance ●

  • Puzzle
  • Physics
  • Mobile

A physics-based puzzle game where players cut structural parts while maintaining stability.

Genre: Physics Puzzle
Platform: Mobile
Year: 2024
Role: Solo Developer
Team Size: Solo Developer
Tools: Unity (C#)

UNIQUE SELLING POINTS

High-stakes risk-reward mechanics; focuses on weight distribution understanding; 'one wrong cut' failure state.

CHALLENGE

Maintaining a stable frame rate while computing real-time physics simulation for multiple structural pieces.

SOLUTION

Limited object count per level and optimized physics collision matrices.

Game Design Breakdown ●

CORE LOOP

Observe -> Cut block -> Stability check -> Maintain balance -> Complete.

LEVEL DESIGN STRATEGY

Progression from basic balance to multi-step planning with unstable materials and complex structures.

TECH CONSTRAINTS

Physics performance optimization; low object-count per level for high FPS.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE

Methodical observation and the 'hold your breath' tension of stability checks.
