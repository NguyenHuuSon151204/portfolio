A cooking and time-management game focused on preparing Vietnamese street food in a high-pressure kitchen — designed for mobile with a full live-ops economy.

2.0 The Concept

INITIAL IDEA

Inspired by Overcooked but localized for Vietnam's street food culture. The player runs a bánh mì stall and must master a 5-step preparation chain under increasing time pressure — with customers losing patience dynamically.

NARRATIVE CONTEXT

No story narrative — the "narrative" is the kinetic satisfaction of the kitchen flow. Each level tells a story through its layout: a cramped family stall vs. a rush-hour tourist spot vs. a catering event.

Kitchen Layout Concept Sketch

UNIQUE SELLING POINTS

Authentic Vietnamese theme; deep prep chain mechanics; full live-ops economy (upgrades, daily missions, rewarded ads) designed with mobile KPIs in mind.

3.0 Game Design Document (GDD)

3.1 Core Loop & System Design

CORE LOOP

Receive order → Prepare ingredients → Assemble bánh mì → Serve → Collect tip → Upgrade station.
Each order has a patience timer (5–12s depending on level). Serving within the green zone yields a bonus tip. Upgrades (faster slicing, bigger bread) are purchased between levels using earned coins — creating a meta-loop that drives retention.

SESSION STRUCTURE

Each level = 1 wave (2–3 minutes). 3 stars for speed + satisfaction score. Designed for mobile: no level lasts longer than 4 minutes to fit commute habits.

RETENTION MECHANICS

Daily login reward → Daily mission → Rewarded Ad at fail state ("Continue for free!") → Weekly event level. All standard mobile LiveOps hooks implemented in prototype.

3.2 Level Design Flow & Pacing

U-SHAPED KITCHEN LAYOUT

All stations arranged in a U-shape so the player's thumb never travels more than 60% of screen height. This reduces cognitive load and enables rapid multi-station chaining.

CHAOS ESCALATION

Order spawn rate dynamically scales with player score: performing well = more orders = more chaos. This creates organic difficulty without hard gates.

Level Layout — U-Shape Kitchen

3.3 UI/UX Breakdown

Every screen was designed in Figma with a 3-tap rule: any key action (order, upgrade, retry) must be reachable in ≤3 taps. The color system uses Vietnamese flag red/yellow as accent to reinforce the cultural theme without clashing with game UI.

Player Journey Wireframe (Figma)

3.4 Economy & Balancing

Dual currency: Coins (earned in-game, used for station upgrades) and Gems (premium, used for cosmetics and continues). Upgrade costs are tuned so the player can upgrade one station per 2–3 completed levels — maintaining a sense of meaningful progression.

4.0 Technical Design Pipeline

3D ASSET OPTIMIZATION

All food items modeled in Blender at <500 tris. Shared material atlas for kitchen props. Mobile draw call budget: <60 per frame on target device (mid-range Android).

TOUCH INPUT DESIGN

Using Unity's EventSystem with custom raycast layers to prevent accidental mis-taps between stations. Minimum touch target size: 88×88px (Apple HIG standard).

Mobile Profiler — Draw Call Optimization
5.0 Key Learnings & Post-Mortem

WHAT DIDN'T WORK

Early levels had 6 available stations simultaneously — playtests showed new players were overwhelmed in the first 30 seconds. Redesigning levels to introduce one station per level dramatically improved the onboarding funnel.

WHAT WORKED WELL

The "Rewarded Ad at fail state" integration was seamless — 70% of simulated players chose the free continue over restarting. This validates the LiveOps hook as a strong retention lever.
