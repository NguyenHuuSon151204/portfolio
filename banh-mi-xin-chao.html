Bánh Mì Xin Chào ●

  • Management
  • Simulation
  • Mobile

A cooking and time-management game focused on preparing Vietnamese street food in a high-pressure kitchen.

Genre: Cooking / Time Management
Platform: Mobile
Year: Jun 2024 - Aug 2024
Role: Game Designer, Level Designer, 2D & 3D Artist
Team Size: 2 (1 Dev, 1 Designer/Artist)
Tools: Unity (C#), Figma

UNIQUE SELLING POINTS

Authentic Vietnamese theme; deep preparation mechanics; challenging multitasking workflows.

CHALLENGE

Handling multi-station workflows and complex recipe multitasking on small touch screens.

SOLUTION

Optimized low-poly 3D models for mobile draw calls and designed a simplified touch-first UI layout.

Game Design Breakdown ●

CORE LOOP

Receive order -> Prepare -> Assemble -> Serve -> Earn tips.

LEVEL DESIGN STRATEGY

Gradual complexity: starting with single steps, then scaling to high volume pressure and station multitasking.

TECH CONSTRAINTS

Low-poly mobile optimization; quick-touch UI design.

PLAYER EXPERIENCE

Satisfaction through frantic efficiency and high-speed coordination.
