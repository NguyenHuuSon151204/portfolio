An addictive Roblox Simulator where players kick Lucky Blocks to hatch Pets, train Stats, and race across zones to hunt mythical rewards.

Genre: Simulator / Casual / Race Tycoon

2.0 Core Loop & Stats Synergy

THE ECONOMIC GEAR

Design Focus: Created a tightly coupled economic gear: Kick Power determines Lucky Block distance ➔ Lucky Blocks yield Pets with progressive Income Multipliers ➔ Earned Cash is reinvested into Training to accelerate progression.

Outcome: Delivered a consistent dopamine loop for casual players. Early sessions are short but intense, while rare Pet hunting (Gacha mechanics) drives long-term retention.

3.0 Monetization & Economy Balancing

3.1 Cross-Progression Ads

FRICTIONLESS MONETIZATION

Design Focus: Implemented a frictionless rewarded ad loop. When players skip grind-heavy quests by watching an ad, the system synchronizes that view to simultaneously credit purely ad-based milestone quests (e.g., Ad Break, Ad Supporter).

Outcome: Naturally boosted Ad Impressions and revenue. Players felt rewarded for "double dipping" quest progress rather than forced into watching ads.

3.2 Economy Balancing & Math Formula

ANTI-SOFT-LOCK FORMULA

Controlled player progression speed to prevent hyper-inflation or economic collapse. Applied an anti-soft-lock formula for daily upgrade quests: Stage_Base_Cash × 30. This balanced the hyper-casual economy scaling and ensured players always had a reachable milestone.

4.0 UI/UX System Design

4.1 Tiered Time-Reward UI

STREAMLINED AFK REWARDS

Design Focus: Consolidated the online time tracker (5m ➔ 15m ➔ 30m) into a single, minimalist UI widget with node milestones, eliminating screen clutter. Claiming a milestone immediately triggers visual feedback on the main chest progress bar.

Outcome: Players received instant gratification for AFK time, effectively increasing the average Session Length per user.

4.2 Anti-Distortion UI Architecture

ADAPTIVE MOBILE GRAPHICS

Design Focus: Fixed text distortion and stretching issues across varied mobile aspect ratios by standardizing TextScaled properties and enforcing UITextSizeConstraint. Applied a 2.5px black UIStroke over a 35% opacity drop-shadow.

Outcome: Numerical stats and cash values remain perfectly square, sharp, and 100% readable on all mobile devices, regardless of camera distance in the 3D space.
