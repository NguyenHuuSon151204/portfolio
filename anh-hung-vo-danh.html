2.0 The Concept

THE CORE FANTASY

"Invisible Power": Inspired by historical Vietnamese resistance fighters, the game strips away direct combat in favor of tactical infiltration. The core player fantasy is built around striking from the shadows—the thrill of outsmarting superior enemy forces by manipulating their environment and reading their behavior.

REGIONAL DEPTH

We designed distinct hero archetypes tied to 5 different regions of Vietnam to intertwine narrative stakes with mechanical progression, ensuring each level feels rooted in a specific historical and geographical context.

UNIQUE SELLING POINTS

Combination of stealth gameplay with puzzle-based progression; culturally-rooted hero characters from different regions of Vietnam with distinct play styles.

3.0 Game Design Document (GDD)

3.1 Core Loop & System Design

SYSTEM-DRIVEN STEALTH

The core loop revolves around the Observe → Plan → Execute sequence. To make the stealth mechanics readable and fair, I implemented a visible Cone of Vision system. This shifts the gameplay from frustrating guesswork to a calculated, deterministic puzzle.

STATE MACHINE AI

The Enemy AI operates on a strict state machine: Patrol → Investigate → Chase → Combat. This predictability allows players to master the systems and feel empowered by their tactical choices.

MECHANICAL WINDOWS

By introducing "Distraction items," players can artificially force the AI into the Investigate state, creating a precise 3-second window to execute a takedown or slip past choke points.

3.2 Level Design Flow & Pacing

LEVEL DESIGN STRATEGY

Each level is designed using a 3-node structure: Safe Zone (tutorial beat) → Tension Zone (multiple patrol routes) → Climax Zone (time pressure + puzzle). This ensures pacing never plateaus.

DIFFICULTY SCALING

Patrol routes narrow and intersect across 5 levels. Level 3 introduces synchronized patrol pairs, eliminating predictable safe windows.

3.3 UI/UX Breakdown

UI design prioritizes information clarity: the Cone of Vision overlay and distraction radius arc are always visible to the player, keeping the stealth read intuitive without a HUD tutorial. All screens follow a dark, ink-on-paper Vietnamese art motif.

3.4 Integrated Mini-Games

To prevent gameplay fatigue, we integrated tactical mini-games (e.g., lock-picking, data retrieval) that bridge the gap between pure stealth and problem-solving.

5.0 Key Learnings & Post-Mortem

THE "RANDOM AI" TRAP

What Didn't Work: In early prototypes, enemy patrol routes included randomized variables. Playtesting revealed this broke the player's ability to plan; players felt punished by RNG rather than their own mistakes.

DETERMINISTIC READABILITY

What Worked: I pivoted to strictly deterministic, looping patrol routes. Combined with the visible Cone of Vision, this turned the game into a learnable puzzle. Key takeaway: In stealth, providing clear systems empowers players to take calculated risks.

4.0 3D Asset & Prop Pipeline

OPTIMIZED ENVIRONMENT PROPS

We established a low-poly asset pipeline ensuring smooth frame rates on WebGL. Each prop follows a PBR (Physically Based Rendering) workflow but is compressed to fit tight browser memory constraints.
